What To Watch For

Symptoms Worth Checking

These concerns are easier to diagnose when you describe exactly when they happen and what changed.

Send Details
Warning Light Appeared Tell the shop which light came on and when it started.
Vehicle Shakes Mention whether it happens at idle, acceleration, braking, or highway speed.
Loss Of Power Reduced acceleration, limp mode, or hesitation should be reviewed.
Hard Starting Slow crank, no crank, clicking, or repeated starting attempts.
Burning Smell Include when the smell appears and whether smoke is visible.
Fluid Under Vehicle Note the colour, location, and whether the level is dropping.
Battery Warning Charging or electrical warnings can affect drivability.
Unusual Noise Describe the noise, speed, and driving condition when it happens.

Can You Diagnose A Check Engine Light?

Yes. Send the code if you already have it, but the shop can also inspect the vehicle and review symptoms.

Should I Keep Driving With A Warning Light?

If the vehicle feels unsafe, overheats, loses power, or has flashing warning lights, call before driving further.

What Details Should I Send?

Include year, make, model, warning lights, sounds, smells, leaks, and when the issue started.

Can Diagnostics Avoid Guessing Parts?

That is the goal. Diagnostics help identify the cause before recommending repair work.
