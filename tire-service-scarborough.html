When To Book Tire Service

Do Not Ignore Tire Or Wheel Issues

Tire and wheel issues can affect braking, steering, and overall safety. If you feel vibration, hear unusual road noise, or notice visible tire damage, book an inspection.

  • Seasonal tire change is due.
  • You feel vibration at certain speeds.
  • Tires are wearing unevenly.
  • You notice a slow leak or low tire pressure.
  • The vehicle pulls to one side.
  • You hear humming, thumping, or unusual road noise.
