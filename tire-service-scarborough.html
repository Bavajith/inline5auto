What To Watch For

Signs You May Need Tire Service

Describe when the tire concern happens and whether it changes with speed, weather, or road surface.

Send Details
Steering Wheel Shake Often noticed at highway speed or after tire changes.
Pressure Warning Tire pressure lights can indicate temperature change or a leak.
Slow Leak Repeated low pressure should be checked.
Uneven Wear Can point to tire, suspension, or alignment-related concerns.
Pulling While Driving May be tire pressure, tire wear, alignment, or suspension related.
