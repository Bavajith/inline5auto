Common Vehicle Problems

Not Sure What’s Wrong?

Send a quick service request if your vehicle has warning lights, brake noise, shaking, leaks, overheating, rough idle, weak A/C, or unusual sounds.

Start Request

Warning Lights

Check engine lights, ABS lights, battery lights, and dashboard alerts.

Brake Noise

Squeaking, grinding, vibration, soft pedal feel, or reduced braking confidence.

Shaking Or Pulling

Steering vibration, uneven tire wear, suspension issues, or alignment-related symptoms.

Leaks Or Smells

Oil, coolant, transmission fluid, fuel smells, burning smells, or visible drips.

Overheating

Temperature warnings, coolant loss, radiator issues, or heating system concerns.

Rough Running

Misfires, rough idle, poor acceleration, stalling, or unusual engine noise.

A/C Not Cold

Weak airflow, no cooling, blower issues, or heating and cooling concerns.

Pre-Trip Inspection

Basic safety checks before long drives, seasonal changes, or daily commuting.
