When To Book Brake Service

Do Not Ignore Brake Warning Signs

Brake issues can become more expensive and unsafe if they are ignored. If something feels off, get the vehicle inspected before the issue gets worse.

  • Squealing, grinding, or scraping when braking.
  • Steering wheel vibration or shaking while stopping.
  • Brake pedal feels soft, low, or different than usual.
  • Vehicle pulls to one side while braking.
  • Brake warning light appears on the dash.
  • Burning smell or overheating near a wheel.

Before You Bring It In

Why Are My Brakes Grinding?

Grinding can mean the brake pads are worn down or another brake component needs attention. Book an inspection.

Can I Drive With Brake Noise?

If braking feels unsafe, call the shop first. Brake noise should be inspected before it becomes a larger safety issue.

What Details Should I Send?

Include your vehicle year, make, model, the sound you hear, when it happens, and whether the brake pedal feels normal.
