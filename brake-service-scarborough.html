What To Watch For

Signs You May Need Brake Service

Brake symptoms should not be ignored. Describe what you hear or feel and when it happens.

Send Details
Grinding Noise Often indicates brake wear that needs prompt inspection.
Squealing Brakes Could be wear indicators, pad condition, or hardware-related noise.
Vibration When Braking May point to rotor, wheel, or suspension-related concerns.
Soft Brake Pedal Can indicate fluid, hydraulic, or air-related issues.
Vehicle Pulls Pulling while braking can come from calipers, tires, or suspension.
Burning Smell A hot smell near wheels may point to sticking brake components.
Brake Warning Light Dashboard warnings should be checked before longer driving.
Longer Stopping Distance Reduced braking performance needs safety-focused inspection.

Can You Check Brake Noise?

Yes. Describe the noise and whether it happens while braking, turning, reversing, or driving.

Should I Drive With Grinding Brakes?

Grinding should be inspected quickly. Call the shop before driving further if the vehicle feels unsafe.

Do Rotors Always Need Replacement?

Not always. It depends on thickness, condition, vibration, corrosion, and wear pattern.

What Details Should I Send?

Include vehicle year, make, model, brake symptoms, noise type, and when it happens.
